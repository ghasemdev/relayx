## 5. Security & Privacy Audit (Constitution Principle III)

- **Default Privacy Masking**: SMS payload is strictly masked upon opening the modal (`••••••••••••`). Unmasking requires deliberate user action on the eye toggle button.
- **Logcat Redaction**: Verified that zero raw message bodies or verification digits appear in Android Logcat during message inspection or retry workflows.
- **Idempotency**: Retrying delivery preserves the exact original message UUID (`c7910f45-c417-4a67-ad21-c0a514c9f78b`), guaranteeing zero duplicate message submissions.
