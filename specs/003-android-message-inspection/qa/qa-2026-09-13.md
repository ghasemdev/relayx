## 3. Security & Privacy Audit (Constitution Principle III)

- **Default Masking**: Message bodies and sensitive OTP codes are strictly masked upon opening the bottom sheet (`••••••••••••`).
- **Explicit Unmasking**: Tapping the eye toggle icon (`content-desc="Reveal text"`) temporarily unmasks the body for the user.
- **Logcat Redaction**: Verified via `grep` and live `logcat` inspection that no raw message payloads or verification digits are logged to Logcat during message inspection.
- **Idempotency**: Retrying delivery preserves the exact original message UUID (`77da93a5-a9c6-4395-b65a-d76d8ce154c3`), guaranteeing zero duplicate message ingestions on the server.
